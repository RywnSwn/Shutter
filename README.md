# Shutter

A little Mac app that hides your stuff.

Pick the apps and websites you want to keep private. Turn on Secure Mode. The moment anyone tries to open one of them, Shutter closes it. They stay closed until you unlock with your password or fingerprint.

Made for the times you hand your laptop to someone who likes clicking around.

## What it does

- Closes any app on your block list the second it opens.
- Closes any browser tab whose website is on your block list.
- Lives quietly in the menu bar. No dock icon, no pop-ups.
- Has a global hotkey so you can lock everything down from any app.
- Unlocks with your password or Touch ID.

## Install

1. Grab the latest version from the [Releases page](https://github.com/RywnSwn/Shutter/releases).
2. Open it and drag **Shutter** into **Applications**.
3. Open Applications, right-click Shutter, and pick **Open**. Only needed the first time, because the app is not signed by Apple.
4. The lock icon appears in your menu bar at the top of the screen.

## First-time setup

When Shutter opens for the first time, a welcome window walks you through:

1. Picking the apps you want to hide.
2. Setting your unlock password.

You can change any of this later from **Settings** in the menu bar menu.

## Using it

- **Turn Secure Mode on:** click the menu bar icon and flip the switch, or press your global hotkey.
- **Turn Secure Mode off:** click the menu bar icon and flip the switch. You'll be asked for your password or fingerprint.
- **Add or remove things from the block list:** open Settings from the menu bar menu.

## A few notes

- Shutter only works while it is running. If you quit it, nothing is blocked. Turn on **Launch at login** in Settings so it starts with your Mac.
- The block list and your password are stored on your Mac only. Nothing leaves your computer.
- Shutter does not lock your whole screen. People can still use your Mac, they just cannot open the things you put on the block list.

## Requirements

- A Mac running macOS 13 or newer.
